## Legal workflow and agent builders

**Harvey (new details).** Since Sep 2025 builders create workflows and request publication approval; Mar 2026 adds Run / View / Edit / Full sharing; on 14 Oct 2026 Harvey let users run custom agents and workflows as soon as they save them, with admin approval only for wider sharing ([release notes](https://help.harvey.ai/release-notes/category/agents)). Natural-language creation: "Words to Workflows" (Jul 2025), design review (Jan 2026), "Magic Builder" (May 2026) (same source). Shared Spaces (Dec 2025) lets firms share Vaults, Workflows and Playbooks with clients, including "self-serve NDA review for clients through custom Workflows" ([blog](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/shared-spaces-and-collaboration-in-harvey), [Feb 2026 post](https://www.harvey.ai/en-US/blog/a-new-era-of-collaboration-for-legal-and-professional-services)). Singapore office opened 2 Jun 2026 with WongPartnership, Rajah & Tann, Drew & Napier, the Attorney-General's Chambers and Airwallex named *(vendor claim)* ([Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/harvey-opens-in-singapore)).

**Legora.** Workflows are "built, customized, and iterated by lawyers without writing a line of code"; building blocks are tools (Tabular Review, research, translation, drafting), reference documents, precedents and style guides; shared library with role-based admin ([product page](https://legora.com/product/workflows)). Tabular Review: documents as rows, questions as columns, typed answers ([help](https://support.legora.com/en/articles/270134-tabular-review)). Playbooks with positions and red lines, mainly in Word ([page](https://legora.com/product/playbooks)). Workflows launched 25 Jun 2025 ([Legal IT Insider](https://legaltechnology.com/?p=76736)). Series E $550M at $8.8B, 140,000+ users *(vendor claim)* ([press release](https://legora.com/newsroom/legora-raises-550-million-series-e-at-8.8-billion-valuation-to-accelerate-growth), [Artificial Lawyer](https://artificiallawyer.com/2026/10/21/legora-raises-550m-at-8-8bn-valuation)). Deloitte Legal sells building Legora workflows and playbooks ([Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/nl/en/services/legal/services/strategic-collaboration-legora-and-deloitte-legal.html)).

**Thomson Reuters CoCounsel.** "Skills perform discrete legal tasks"; workflows "combine multiple skills" ([help](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/help/cocounsel/legal/navigation/skills-and-workflows/skills-prompts-workflows)). Guided workflows launched Aug 2025 ([press](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en/press-releases/2025/august/thomson-reuters-launches-cocounsel-legal-transforming-legal-work-with-agentic-ai-and-deep-research)). Next-generation CoCounsel Legal is "built on Anthropic Agent SDK"; firms configure their own workflows and import playbooks into Word; beta as of Jun 2026 ([TR post](https://www.thomsonreuters.com/en-us/posts/innovation/mays-cocounsel-legal-releases/)).

**LexisNexis Protégé.** US GA 24 Feb 2026 with a no-code Custom Workflow Builder to "design, test, and share" workflows, sold with a "white glove" build service ([press](https://www.lexisnexis.com/community/amp-pressroom/general-availability-of-lexis-with-protege-sets-new-standard-for-automating-legal-work-with-easy-to-use-authoritative-ai-workflows), [LawSites](https://www.lawnext.com/2026/02/lexisnexis-launches-lexis-with-protege-replacing-lexis-ai-with-an-end-to-end-workflow-platform.html)). Hong Kong launch of "Skills" (Aug 2026): guided workflows such as written resolutions, DPA review, PIA generation, incorporating LexisNexis and Anthropic skills and the CaseBase HK citator; APAC survey: 71.5% of HK respondents would use AI more if delivered as guided workflows *(vendor survey)* ([LN HK](https://www.lexisnexis.com/blogs/hk-legal/b/lexisnexis-solutions/posts/lexisnexis-launches-skills-in-lexis-with-protege-in-hong-kong-advancing-the-legal-ai-solution-built-on-the-authority-legal-work-demands)).

**Clio / vLex Vincent Studio.** Enterprise-only; "designated publishers" build and publish to the organisation or groups ([vLex](https://vlex.com/news/vincent-studio-ai-that-works-the-way-you-do)); wizard Details → Build → Tasks → Publish, with workflow assets (playbooks, checklists, templates) and immediate or suggested tasks ([guide](https://support.vlex.com/vincent-by-vlex-1/vincent-studio/creating-and-managing-ai-workflows-in-vincent-studio/how-to-create-a-new-workflow-in-vincent-studio-4-step-guide), [architecture](https://support.vlex.com/vincent-by-vlex-1/vincent-studio/getting-started-with-vincent-studio-workflows/understanding-the-vincent-studio-architecture-workflows-tasks-and-steps)). Clio blog dated 26 Feb 2026 ([Clio](https://www.clio.com/enterprise/blog/vincent-studio-ai-that-works-the-way-you-do/)).

**August.** Self-serve launch Jan 2026 with a free trial and 100+ tutorials; playbooks generated from the firm's precedents ([LawSites](https://www.lawnext.com/2026/01/aiming-to-make-ai-more-easily-accessible-for-smaller-law-firms-legal-ai-company-august-launches-self-service-platform-and-free-educational-library.html), [Artificial Lawyer](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2026/01/27/august-launches-self-serve-video-tutorial-academy/)).

**anthropics/claude-for-legal.** Apache-2.0, launched May 2026 with 12 practice plugins ([repo](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-for-legal), [LawSites](https://lawnext.com/2026/05/anthropic-goes-all-in-on-legal-releasing-more-than-20-connectors-and-12-practice-area-plugins-for-claude.html)). Each skill is a `SKILL.md`; a 10–20 minute "cold-start interview" reads seed documents and writes a practice profile that other skills read; every output is "a draft for attorney review", model-only citations are flagged `[verify]`, and there are explicit gates before anything is sent.

**Others.** Wordsmith rolls out intake → playbooks → automation → business self-service *(vendor claim)* ([Sacra](https://sacra.com/c/wordsmith/), [Series B](https://www.newswire.com/news/wordsmith-raises-70m-to-bring-legal-work-back-in-house-and-away-from-law-firms)). Flank answers business teams in Slack, email and Teams against playbooks ([Insight Partners](https://insightpartners.com/ideas/flank-raises-10m-to-scale-autonomous-legal-agents-embedded-invisible-and-built-for-the-enterprise)).

**Inference — cross-vendor pattern.** A small builder group (KM, innovation, senior lawyer) builds; many people run. Building blocks: goal, reference assets, typed inputs, steps, human checkpoints, Word or table output. Governance: builder → optional approval → shared at an access level. Harvey's Oct 2026 change shows approval friction is a real adoption cost.

## What "Skill" and "Workflow" mean in current products

- Anthropic Agent Skills (16 Oct 2025): "organized folders of instructions, scripts, and resources", loaded progressively, composable ([Anthropic engineering](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/equipping-agents-for-the-real-world-with-agent-skills)).
- Anthropic, "Building effective agents": workflows are LLMs and tools "orchestrated through predefined code paths"; agents direct their own process ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents)). Harvey moved from Workflow Builder (fixed steps) to Agent Builder ([Harvey](https://www.harvey.ai/blog/introducing-agent-builder)).
- OpenAI: custom GPTs retire 11 Dec 2026; a migrated GPT's instructions become a skill and its knowledge files become references ([Virtualization Review](https://virtualizationreview.com/articles/2026/09/28/openai-to-retire-custom-gpts-replace-them-with-plugins.aspx), [OpenAI FAQ](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001519)). AgentKit Agent Builder has nodes for guardrails and user approval, preview runs, evals and versioning ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-agentkit/)).
- **Inference.** Define the terms up front: a Skill is one reusable task with declared inputs, instructions, references and an output contract; a Workflow is an ordered composition with explicit data passing and human checkpoints.

## Singapore and Hong Kong context

- **MinLaw, Guide for Using Generative AI in the Legal Sector (6 Mar 2026).** Principles: professional ethics, confidentiality, transparency; audience includes law firms, in-house counsel and legal technologists; "apply necessary and proportionate human oversight" with a risk-based diagram; tool due diligence asks about audit trails, citations, reasoning, no training on firm data, data residency, version histories and approval logs ([launch](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/launch-of-guide-for-using-generative-artificial-intelligence-in-the-legal-sector/), [PDF](https://www.mlaw.gov.sg/files/Final_Ver__Guide_for_using_Generative_AI_in_the_Legal_Sector.pdf)). ## Commercial synthesis

**Inference.** AltaLex's Team tier sells "Practice-specific" and "Customized Workflows (Optional)" at custom pricing ([pricing](https://alta-lex.ai/pricing)). Peers productise customisation while still selling build help: LexisNexis "white glove", Deloitte on Legora, Ironclad Services for Jurist. Self-serve lowers the marginal cost of each customised customer, lets smaller teams buy without a services contract (August's move), keeps the team's playbook in the product, and opens firm-to-client distribution (Harvey Shared Spaces). Since Harvey opened in Singapore (Jun 2026) and LexisNexis launched HK Skills (Aug 2026), AltaLex's defensible angle is cross-border Chinese/English work at a published self-serve price.

## Likely objections

- "Lawyers won't build workflows." One or two builders per team is the documented pattern (Vincent publishers, Spellbook authors); creation is moving to interviews and natural language (Harvey Words to Workflows, claude-for-legal cold-start).
- "Harvey already does this." It confirms demand; local jurisdiction content, bilingual output and price are the differentiators in SG/HK.
- "User-built Skills will hallucinate." The MinLaw and Courts guides put responsibility on the lawyer; the product must offer sample tests, source-linked output and verification flags.
- "Business users self-serving legal advice is risky." Lawyers build and own the Skills; business staff only run them (Wordsmith, Flank pattern; HK DoJ warning).
